Ascentrix and CloudLink Case Study

Ascentrix, a large multinational technology company, acquired CloudLink, a mid-sized cloud solutions provider, to combine CloudLink’s technical expertise and innovation with Ascentrix’s structured processes and ability to scale. The aim is to expand into emerging cloud technologies and strengthen its market presence

Challenges and proposed solutions

Five challenges put the merger at risk. As the business analyst, I proposed a solution for each:

  • Misaligned priorities: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound (SMART) goals based on the merger objectives.
  • Unclear roles: a Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed (RACI) matrix covering 17 integration tasks, built on defined roles and a stakeholder power and interest grid of 18 stakeholder groups.
  • Ineffective communication: a communication plan, with the stakeholder grid showing how closely to engage each group.
  • No way to measure success: performance indicators linked to the SMART goals.
  • Disparate systems and teams: an audit of both companies’ systems and processes, followed by process alignment and system migration.

Outcome

Together, these give both organisations a shared direction and clear accountability.

RetailMart Case Study

RetailMart is experiencing declining customer satisfaction, which has negatively affected sales. Key challenges include long service wait times, limited personalized assistance, and inadequate product knowledge among sales staff. As the business analyst, I will use appropriate tools to conduct a gap analysis for RetailMart. The analysis will identify the company's current challenges, define its desired future state, and determine the actions needed to achieve it. The steps to take in performing this analysis are seen in the image below:

Challenges and proposed solutions

As the business analyst, I performed a Gap Analysis to define the current state of the business using a Fishbone Diagram. The current state was divided into six categories: Man, Method, Measurement, Material, Machine and Environment. The causes of the challenges were identified. The expected state of RetailMart was also examined. Finally, the actions required to get the business to its expected state were studied. For example, the present state of lack of product knowledge among the sales staff can be resolved by running onboarding and regular product training for the staff.

Outcome

Each challenge that was identified has a corresponding solution which, when executed, will bring RetailMart to the expected state.

Skills Demonstrated

Problem solving. Gap Analysis, Fishbone Diagram. Tools: Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Word, Power Point.
