Hospital
Business and Operations Analysis

The Hospital is a reputable healthcare provider operating across several locations. It holds a substantial amount of data covering financial transactions, hospital procedures, and patient and doctor records. At present, however, decisions are largely driven by intuition rather than evidence, which leaves valuable opportunities unrealised. Management wants to draw meaningful insights from this data, understand key trends, and use those insights to make decisions that support the hospital's growth.

PROBLEM STATEMENT AND SOLUTION

City Hospital currently lacks a centralised view of its financial performance. Without one, management cannot easily identify trends, optimise the allocation of resources, or make consistent, data-driven decisions. This project addresses that gap by bringing the hospital's data together into a single, clear view. As the Data Analyst on this project, my role is to help City Hospital make more strategic, data-driven decisions across both finance and operations.
